05 — About

Built from
scratch. In public.

GeoSim FUTO is a geospatial data science project that reconstructs the terrain around the Federal University of Technology Owerri (FUTO), Nigeria, from sparse VES elevation survey data.

The project uses Inverse Distance Weighting (IDW) interpolation — selected through LOOCV validation — to generate a continuous terrain surface, then derives slope, aspect, terrain classification, flood susceptibility, erosion susceptibility, and urban expansion pressure layers from that single surface.

Final outputs include interactive 3D Plotly viewers, AR/VR-ready exports (.obj mesh, PNG texture maps), and generative AI scene prompts grounded in real GIS data.
